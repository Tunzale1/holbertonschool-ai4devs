# MVP Concept – Plastic2Future AZ

## 1. Problem Statement

Urban areas in Azerbaijan, particularly Baku, face challenges with plastic waste collection, sorting, and recycling. Many residents do not know where to dispose of recyclable plastic, while recycling businesses often struggle to access reliable information about available recyclable materials.

Plastic2Future AZ is an AI-powered digital platform designed to connect citizens, waste collectors, and recycling businesses. It aims to make plastic recycling more accessible, encourage responsible waste disposal, and support a circular economy by turning plastic waste into reusable resources.

## 2. Target Users

- **Residents and Citizens:** People who want to dispose of plastic waste responsibly and find nearby collection points.
- **Waste Collectors:** Individuals or organizations involved in collecting and transporting recyclable plastic.
- **Recycling Businesses:** Companies that purchase, process, and recycle plastic materials.
- **Environmental Organizations:** Organizations interested in monitoring and supporting waste-reduction initiatives.

## 3. Core Features

1. **Plastic Collection Point Map:** Allows users to find nearby plastic recycling and collection locations.

2. **AI Plastic Identification:** Allows users to upload a photo of plastic waste and receive an AI-generated identification of its likely plastic type and recycling category.

3. **Collection Tracking:** Enables users to record plastic waste collection activities and track the estimated amount of plastic collected.

4. **Recycling Marketplace:** Connects plastic collectors with recycling businesses interested in purchasing recyclable materials.

5. **Environmental Impact Dashboard:** Displays estimated plastic collected, recycling activity, and environmental impact statistics.

## 4. Constraints

- The initial MVP will focus on Baku, Azerbaijan.
- The first version will be a web application rather than a mobile application.
- User authentication will be optional or excluded from the initial prototype.
- AI plastic identification will provide estimates and may not always be accurate.
- Collection point information will initially be limited to manually added locations.
- The marketplace will facilitate connections but will not process payments.
- The platform will not operate physical recycling facilities or collection stations.
- The MVP will use a limited dataset and a small number of recycling partners.
- Environmental impact figures will be estimates rather than independently verified measurements.

## 5. MVP Success Criteria

- Users can locate plastic collection points.
- Users can upload images and receive plastic identification results.
- Users can record plastic collection activities.
- Recycling businesses can view available recyclable materials.
- Users can view basic environmental impact statistics.

The initial objective is to validate whether a digital platform can make plastic recycling more accessible and improve connections between waste collectors and recycling businesses.
